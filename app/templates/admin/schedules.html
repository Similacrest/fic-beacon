Schedules

Each schedule is a release time (cron) plus how much it may release in total — e.g. 5,000 words on weekday mornings, 10,000 on weekday evenings, 20,000 at the weekend. A release's budget is split across the channels that have something to drop, in proportion to their weight. Times use the server's timezone.
